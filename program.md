# Research Program

Produce falsifiable, evidence-linked research artifacts. Generated prose is not
evidence. Preserve null, negative, failed, invalid, and rejected work. Never
claim novelty, correctness, venue readiness, or acceptance from model judgment
alone. Verified empirical findings may be stated as contributions when every
supporting claim round-trips through the evidence ledger; model-generated prose
must not introduce claims absent from the ledger, and contribution framing must
be derived from the experimental evidence rather than the model's world
knowledge. Stop and report a blocker when source, license, protocol, budget, or
provenance requirements are unresolved.
