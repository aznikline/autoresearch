A. Causal Semantic Intervention (recommended)

LLM constructs identity-preserving nuisance interventions for camera, background, illumination, pose, and clothing cues. A frozen VLM scores whether the representation changes for the right reasons; a small student learns intervention consistency.

Pros

  • Mechanism-level claim
  • Fits cross-domain generalization
  • Image-only inference
  • Can test with frozen backbones

Risks

  • Must prove interventions are identity-preserving
  • Causal language must be carefully scoped
  • Needs strong semantic ablations

B. Adaptive Semantic Prototype Bank

Generate a controlled vocabulary of body parts, appearance attributes, and nuisance factors; learn a tiny router that selects prototypes for each image while distilling them into a ReID embedding.

Pros

  • Cheapest and simplest
  • Interpretable prototype activations
  • Easy Colab implementation

Risks

  • Very close to 2026 multi-grained alignment and ALADIN
  • Likely incremental without a new learning principle

C. Semantic Hard-Negative Curriculum

Use VLM captions and LLM reasoning to explain why visually similar identities differ, then convert explanations into pairwise constraints and a curriculum for a lightweight student.

Pros

  • Low training cost
  • Directly targets ReID errors
  • Clear retrieval diagnostics

Risks

  • Hallucinated differences can leak labels
  • Contribution may look like data mining
  • Requires strict pair provenance
