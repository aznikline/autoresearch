A. Group-Relative Intervention Optimization (recommended)

Sample a group of LLM-generated interventions per identity. Use rule-based retrieval, identity-preservation, diversity, and semantic-validity rewards. Normalize rewards inside the group, update a tiny intervention policy with reference anchoring, then distill accepted trajectories into the image adapter.

Why it transfers

  • Same group-relative credit assignment as reasoning RL
  • ReID rank gives a verifiable reward
  • No critic or LLM fine-tuning
  • Fits free Colab

Main risk

  • Reward hacking through trivial augmentations
  • Must beat hard-negative and consistency baselines

B. Multi-Intervention Prediction

Predict several counterfactual embeddings or semantic changes from each source image, densifying supervision in the spirit of multi-token prediction.

Pros

  • Simple differentiable training
  • Good auxiliary objective
  • Low compute

Risk

  • May be judged as ordinary multi-task consistency
  • Weak as the sole contribution

C. Auxiliary-Loss-Free Semantic Experts

Route visual tokens to nuisance-specific experts and balance utilization by bias updates rather than an auxiliary loss.

Pros

  • Direct architectural transfer
  • Potentially interpretable specialization

Risk

  • Recent ReID expert-routing overlap
  • Higher memory and ablation burden
  • Poor fit for zero budget

Recommended paper shape

A is the core algorithm. B is included only as a small auxiliary head if the ablation proves it adds independent value. C is excluded from v1.
