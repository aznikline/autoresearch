Evidence plan: zero-budget discovery to submission candidate

DukeMTMC-ReID is excluded. Every comparison is rerun under the same Duke-free protocol.

Dataset protocol

single-source cross-domain pairs
Publication

Required baseline ladder
  1. Frozen OpenCLIP feature baseline.
  2. Same adapter with identity classification + triplet loss.
  3. Same compute with standard color/blur/erasing augmentation.
  4. Random intervention bank + consistency loss.
  5. Hard intervention selection without group-relative optimization.
  6. GRIO without VLM validation; GRIO without diversity; full GRIO.
  7. At least one reproducible recent DG-ReID baseline under the same Duke-free data.
Primary evidence
  • mAP and Rank-1
  • Mean ± 95% bootstrap CI over seeds
  • Cross-domain average and worst target
  • Adapter parameters, wall time, peak VRAM
  • Policy entropy and intervention acceptance rate
Falsification tests
  • Shuffled LLM programs
  • Generic augmentations at equal budget
  • Invalid/destructive intervention rate
  • In-domain accuracy tradeoff
  • Different LLM/VLM teacher sensitivity
  • Reward-component ablations
Venue routing (sequential, never simultaneous)
CVPR / ECCV / ICCV

ICLR
Only if GRIO generalizes beyond ReID and the optimization principle is the central contribution.
NeurIPS
Only with broader retrieval evidence, deeper analysis, and a generally useful learning formulation.
