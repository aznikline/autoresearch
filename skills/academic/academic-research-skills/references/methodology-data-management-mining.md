# Data Management And Mining Methodology

Record database/system versions, schema, workload/query set, scale, indexes,
optimizer and runtime configuration, hardware/storage, cache, warm-up,
concurrency, isolation, repetitions, and timeouts. Use tuned strong baselines on
matched resources. Report latency distributions and tails, throughput,
correctness, failures, memory, CPU, I/O, energy, and cost. For predictive or
ranking work, also freeze temporal splits and prevent feature/label leakage.
