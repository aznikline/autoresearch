# Autoresearch Foundation Models And LLMs

Treat generated claims and model-judge scores as proposals, not evidence. Freeze
model versions, prompts, decoding, datasets, evaluators, and budgets before
confirmatory work. Preserve samples, judge outputs, failures, safety outcomes,
latency, token use, and cost. Block broad capability or novelty claims when
contamination, judge validity, or provenance remains unresolved.
