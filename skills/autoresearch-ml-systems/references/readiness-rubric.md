# Submission Readiness Rubric

Block `submission_ready` unless all applicable checks have structured evidence:

1. Claims match scope, assumptions, and limitations.
2. The literature search covers nearest work and records unresolved novelty risk.
3. Baselines are strong and protocol/resource matched.
4. Evaluation units span the claimed generalization scope.
5. Primary metrics, seeds, exclusions, and stopping rules were fixed before the
   confirmatory run.
6. Results include effect sizes, correctly defined uncertainty, null outcomes,
   failed runs, and quality-efficiency tradeoffs.
7. Per-run and total compute and a reproducible environment/command are present.
8. Every citation and numeric claim resolves to a registry entry.
9. Code, configs, raw outputs, and the artifact manifest are complete.
10. Every hypothesis is supported, refuted, or inconclusive.

Numeric profile floors are operational defaults. Accept a waiver only when it
names the affected claim, explains why the floor is inapplicable, and supplies
an alternative test. Never waive provenance, immutable evaluation, citation
integrity, or comparison parity.
